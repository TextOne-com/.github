### 🎯 Our mission

**A good textbook doesn't just teach — it accompanies.** We create textbook series for
preschool, primary and secondary that put understanding and critical thinking ahead of
memorisation, aligned with the Colombian curriculum.

Our vision is to be the reference publisher for Colombian schools looking for curriculum-aligned
series with real pedagogical support. We treat schools as partners, not customers.

### 📦 What every series includes

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 📘 | **Student book** | The full curriculum content, designed for everyday work in class. |
| ✏️ | **Workbook** | Practice and reinforcement aligned with each unit of the book. |
| 🧑‍🏫 | **Teacher guide** | Lesson planning, formative assessment and teaching guidance for every unit. |
| 💻 | **Digital platform** | Complementary resources and progress tracking, in the secondary series. |

### 🤝 How we work with schools

We don't just sell a book: we support its implementation, from the first conversation to the
classroom.

1. **Tell us about your school** — your context and your curriculum needs.
2. **We present the series** — an advisor walks you through the teaching approach and the
   materials that fit best.
3. **We support the implementation** — teacher guides, formative assessment and ongoing support
   throughout the school year.

### 🌱 What guides us

- **Pedagogical rigour** — every series follows a clear, curriculum-aligned teaching approach.
- **Closeness** — clear, warm language for teachers, families and students.
- **Support** — guides, platforms and direct advice, beyond the book itself.
- **Growth** — learning as a long-term process, not an immediate result.

### 👩‍👧 Who we write for

**Schools and teachers**, who evaluate and adopt complete series · **Families**, who support
learning at home · **Students**, for whom every page should feel clear, engaging and easy to
follow.
